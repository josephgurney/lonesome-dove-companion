## Conflicts found (text vs. study guides)

In every case below, the text was followed.

**Chapter numbering and coverage**
- GradeSaver's "Chapters 1–15" page ends at ch 11, so ch 12–15 have no GradeSaver summary.
- TheBestNotes merges or mislabels some chapters: the ch 21 page includes ch 22's opening, and ch 47 and ch 49 have no labels of their own. Its "ch 46" includes events that are in ch 47.

**Chapters 1–15**
- ch 1: GradeSaver calls the barber "Dillan"; the text says Dillard Brawley.
- ch 4: BestNotes says Gus refuses Dish's $2 loan. He actually gives it after the card game.
- ch 7: BestNotes says Gus goes to visit Lorena. He only thinks about it, then goes to watch Call work the mare.
- ch 1 vs ch 11: in ch 1 Gus offers to lend Newt "my Henry", but ch 11 says Call carries the only Henry on the border. This is an inconsistency inside the novel.
- ch 2 vs ch 9: Gus's pocket watch is silver in ch 2 and brass in ch 9 (also internal to the novel).

**Chapters 16–46**
- ch 17: GradeSaver says "a few weeks"; the text says ten days.
- ch 20: GradeSaver says Jake agrees to go to Montana. He only agrees to ride alongside the herd, camping separately, and talks of Denver.
- ch 24: the text mentions a "Charlie Rainey", but ch 16 hired Jimmy and Ben. This is probably an inconsistency in the novel.
- ch 26: GradeSaver gives July the fondness for Jake over his brother Ben; in the text that is Roscoe.
- ch 29: GradeSaver says Peach tells Roscoe to chase Elmira. In the text old lady Harkness suggests it, and Peach sends him after July.
- ch 30–31: GradeSaver says Deets removes "the needle" (he uses a needle to remove the thorn), and that Dish warns Newt about lightning on the cattle (it is Deets).
- ch 36: the edition prints the chief whiskey trader as "Bowler" once and "Fowler" elsewhere, and once has "Port Smith" for Fort Smith.
- ch 42: TheBestNotes and Shmoop say a cook is hired in San Antonio. None is; Po Campo is found near Austin (ch 46–47).
- ch 44: Clara's orchard is a spring-fed creek and grove near a Guadalupe tributary, off the route toward Austin.

**Chapters 47–74**
- ch 47: GradeSaver says Pea Eye brings Mouse back to Newt. In the text Pea only reports that the horse turned up in the herd.
- ch 49: the red-sand river Blue Duck crosses is not named there. Ch 56 establishes that it is the Red.
- ch 51: Wilbarger calls it "summertime", but ch 54 and 58 call it spring. This is internal to the novel.
- ch 53: GradeSaver says Fowler brings "Janey" food; it is Elmira. Shmoop says Big Zwey isn't a buffalo hunter; he is.
- ch 54: GradeSaver says Blue Duck always retreated to the Palo Duro. The text says the country north and west of it.
- ch 56: Shmoop says some of Gus's attackers were white men. They are Indians, joined later by one white buffalo hunter, Dog Face.
- ch 59: the herd is held west of Fort Worth and never enters the town; the hands ride in.
- ch 61: TheBestNotes has Gus remembering soldiers at Adobe Walls; that isn't in the text.
- ch 62: the stampede broke west; Newt ends up with strays and the main herd is six or seven miles east of him, so both guides are partly right.
- ch 67: GradeSaver says the herd goes north of the grasshopper swarm; the text turns it west, toward water.
- ch 68: Jake's shooting takes place at Doan's Store on the Red River.
- ch 69: GradeSaver calls Dee Boot "Dee Brown".
- ch 71: GradeSaver says Dan Suggs shoots Frog Lip; in the text it is Eddie.
- ch 72: GradeSaver says Dish's horse panicked at the smell of Wilbarger's blood; the text gives no reason.

**Chapters 75–102**
- ch 76: GradeSaver says "the sheriff" wakes Dee; the text names the deputy, Leon.
- ch 83 and 87: GradeSaver and Shmoop say Captain Weaver reports Elmira and Zwey dead. Weaver only mentions a buffalo hunter and a woman; soldiers confirm the news to Clara's household later (ch 87).
- ch 75 vs 82: Bob Allen's injury is three months old in one place and two months in another, and Clara's aunt lives in Cincinnati in one chapter and Richmond in another. Both are internal to the novel.
- ch 89–90: GradeSaver and Shmoop say "Salt River", and BestNotes says the Powder. After the dry drive the herd reaches **Salt Creek** (Wyoming) and gets to the Powder later.
- ch 90: GradeSaver says six horses were stolen; the text says twelve. GradeSaver has the men regret not calling Deets "Joshua"; the text says "Josh".
- ch 93: GradeSaver says Gus dislikes Montana. He actually calls it rare country and says he wouldn't have missed it.
- ch 94: GradeSaver says the war party was 40; the text says at least twenty. TheBestNotes names the attackers as Utes, which the text never does. Hugh Auld says Bloods (ch 95).
- ch 94–96: Gus and Pea are not attacked on the Yellowstone itself. It happens on a creek three or more days north of it, which Call later calls the Musselshell.
- ch 100: the young horse thief is Tom, nicknamed "Big Tom" (TheBestNotes is correct here).
- ch 48 vs ch 100: Jerry is a sorrel in ch 48 and a dun in ch 100 (inconsistent inside the novel).
- ch 102: GradeSaver says Call rode through Denver to avoid crowds. He actually turned into Colorado to avoid the cattle trails, and Denver drew a crowd.
- ch 102: GradeSaver says Call was shot "in New Mexico". The text puts the ambush a day above Horsehead Crossing on the Pecos, just inside Texas; LitCharts also says New Mexico plains.
- ch 102: GradeSaver says Call carried Gus on the mule. He built a travois from the sign.
- ch 102: Shmoop calls Dillard Brawley "a cowboy" and says Bolivar never went home. Bolivar did go home to Mexico and came back.
- Burial place: Wikipedia says Gus is buried in "San Antonio". The text says Clara's orchard, a live-oak hill above the (south) Guadalupe; settlers there head down toward San Antonio.
